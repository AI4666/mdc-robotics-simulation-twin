🏗️ Methodology: Multi-Method Architecture
🗣️ Speaker: Jorge A Mendoza (2:00 – 3:30)
Unifying Discrete-Event (DES) & Agent-Based Modeling (ABM)
Capturing transactional order flow alongside physical spatial kinematics and battery physics
📊 Why Multi-Method is Essential
  • DES Limitations Alone: Treats delays as abstract numbers; ignores floorplan wall collisions, turning radii, and physical docking contention.
  • ABM Limitations Alone: Struggles with transactional ticket queues, backlogs, and SLA timeout timers.
  • The Hybrid Fusion: In AnyLogic 8, student work orders (DES Entities) dynamically Seize physical AMRs (ABM Agents) that physically navigate the floorplan layout!

Solving the Multi-Objective Dispatch & Recharging MDP

🎯 Multi-Objective Reward Function
R = +10•N_done - 0.5•ΣQueues - 25•I(SOC ≤ 5%) - 2.0•DockWait

Balances high completed deliveries against queue penalties and heavily punishes mid-aisle battery exhaustion ($0\%$).

💡 PPO's Key Breakthrough: Opportunistic Charging

Traditional heuristics only charge when $SOC < 20\%$. PPO discovers that taking short 5-minute top-offs when queues are empty ($Q \le 1$), even at 65% battery, eliminates simultaneous fleet depletion!

💡 Non-Obvious Insights & Actionable Takeaways
🗣️ Speaker: Ricardo Molina Gonzalez (9:45 – 11:00)
What Did the Simulation Reveal That Was Not Obvious?
Uncovering hidden system traps and providing actionable directives for lab management
⚠️ The "Fleet Sizing Trap"

Intuition suggests that buying 5 AMRs would clear orders faster than 3 AMRs. The simulation proved the exact opposite: adding 5 robots caused physical corridor congestion in the 1.8m passage between Stations A and B, increasing wait times by 14%!

⚡ The "Charging Cascade" Pitfall

Simple threshold charging (charge when <20%) creates coordinated battery exhaustion. Because all robots deploy at 8:00 AM, they all hit 20% simultaneously at 11:30 AM, shutting down the lab during peak class hours.

🎯 Actionable Recommendations for MDC Leadership
1. Cap Fleet Size at 3 AMRs: Avoid wasteful capital expenditures on excess hardware; 3 units maximize throughput.
2. Deploy PPO Staggered Charging: Recharge during brief queue dips rather than waiting for critical battery exhaustion.
3. Dedicated Station C Corridor: Designate exclusive right-of-way zones near QA testing to avoid cross-traffic deadlock.
