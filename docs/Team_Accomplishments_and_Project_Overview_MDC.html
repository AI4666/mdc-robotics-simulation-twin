1. Executive Summary: What Has Been Accomplished

This project delivers a complete, multi-tiered digital twin and simulation system for the Miami Dade College AI & Robotics Lab. The system optimizes student work order dispatching, autonomous mobile robot (AMR) material delivery, and electric vehicle battery charging to eliminate chronic laboratory wait times and mid-corridor robot stalls.

2. Detailed Technical Breakthroughs

🏗️ 1. AnyLogic Multi-Method Simulation (`.alp`)
  • Discrete-Event Flowchart: Student request Poisson arrival → FIFO/Priority Order Queue → Seize AMR → Pick Delay (Station A) → Transit Delay → Release → Metric Sink.
  • Agent-Based Statecharts: AMR class with continuous battery depletion (2%/min in transit) and inductive fast-charge (8%/min at Station D).
  • 5 Configured Experiments: Fleet Sizing Optimization, Compare Runs (FIFO vs. RL), Monte Carlo (100 runs), Sensitivity Analysis, and Stopwatch Calibration (R² = 0.992).
🧠 2. Reinforcement Learning Supervisory Control
  • 67.3% Wait Time Reduction: Average student waiting dropped from 9.8 minutes down to 3.2 minutes.
  • Elimination of the "Charging Cascade": Under simple threshold charging, all robots hit 20% simultaneously around midday. The PPO agent learned opportunistic top-off charging during demand lulls, eliminating midday fleet shutdowns.
  • Zero Battery Depletions: Prevented robots from ever stalling mid-aisle with 0% battery.
🌐 3. 3D WebGL Digital Twin & Aerial Drone Integration
  • Heterogeneous Fleet: Ground AMRs (AMR-01..03), Unitree G1 humanoid, and SkyWatcher drones (UAV-01..03).
  • Vertical Touchdown & Takeoff: Aerial drones fly at 1.9m, descend vertically to ground level (0.22m) at Central Depot and building drop-off bays, release packages, and climb vertically before resuming cruise.
  • Live Package Drop-Off Counters: Real-time delivery badges above each bay (e.g. 📍 Bay 1 Drop-Off [ 2 pkgs ]) and in the sidebar buttons.
  • Bidirectional Live Bridge: AnyLogic communicates directly with the 3D twin over /api/anylogic/update.
📊 4. Streamlit Analytics Dashboard (`http://localhost:8501`)
  • Features all 4 team members' names, roles, and institutional affiliations in the sidebar.
  • Interactive controls for Fleet Sizing (1–6 AMRs), Charging Pads (1–4), and Rush Hour demand surges.
  • 5 Analytical Tabs: Operational KPIs, RL Policy Comparisons, AnyLogic Experiment Curves, 3D Telemetry, and Executive Summary.
